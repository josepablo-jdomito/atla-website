01

Fix thisFinding 1 of 3 · Instagram

Your Instagram bio holds the best line kiimento owns.

Why it matters

It answers why kiimento and not another kids camera. The site never says it.

Where to take it

Make this idea the first thing the site says, and build the rest of the page around it.

If it stays

Parents compare kiimento to cheaper cameras on price, because the site gives them no other reason.

02

Fix thisFinding 2 of 3 · Website

On the site the same idea shrinks to a sticker on a photo.

Why it matters

The reason to pay more than a toy camera reads as decoration.

Where to take it

Give the promise the weight of a headline, not a badge.

If it stays

The reason to pay more gets read as decoration and skipped.

03

OpportunityFinding 3 of 3 · Instagram

The grid sells a licensed toy, not a first camera that is theirs.

Why it matters

Collabs bring reach, but a grid of someone else's character makes kiimento look like a toy shop.

Where to take it

Keep collabs as moments and let the children's own photos be the grid.

If it stays

Every unboxing builds a licensed character instead of kiimento.

One brand to look at

Moleskine, from another category: it sells the notebook as the place your ideas live, not as paper.
