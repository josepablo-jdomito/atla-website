## Body (one paragraph = one block)

TigreTigre is a sushi and sake restaurant on Avenida Chapultepec in San Luis Potosí, opened by José Pablo and partners in 2023. The pitch fits on a sticker: after-office sake and food. Japanese comfort plates with good ingredients, cocktails, premium sake, a place to land when the day ends. The brand was made before the doors opened.

A restaurant with two moods, casual and formal, needs a brand that can be both. The name already repeats itself. The identity had to turn that repetition into a character, and keep a place that serves ramen on Tuesdays and author plates on Saturdays from looking like two restaurants.

The wordmark mixes two type styles, one casual and one formal, so the name moves. The icon is a tiger with two heads sharing one eye: duality and repetition, straight from the name. A kanji mark, 虎虎, for the small places. And a sticker voice for the rest: Sake it off. Roll with it. Have a rice day.

Logotype and three variants, the two-headed tiger, the kanji mark, color and type. Stationery and cards, stickers and labels, menu, posters and flyers, bag and packaging mock, social templates and the first website design.

The restaurant is open, into its third year, with a new menu for 2026.

## We would say this to a CMO

- If the name repeats, make the repetition the idea.
- A restaurant brand lives on stickers and menus more than on a logo. Write the stickers first.

### The idea chosen and why
- Wordmark: "El logotipo se compone de un wordmark que juega con dos estilos de tipografía diferentes, representando la dualidad de la marca entre lo casual y algo formal, creando también un dinamismo que le brinda movimiento y personalidad. A partir de este logotipo nacen otras tres versiones que pueden ser aplicadas de igual manera.", brand book p.5, 2023-04-05.
- Icon: "El wordmark es acompañado de un icono que muestra un tigre de dos cabezas que comparten un ojo, explotando el concepto de la dualidad y la repetición en el mismo nombre de la marca. Se propone su uso en cuerpo completo, así como solamente las cabezas separadas.", brand book p.6, 2023-04-05.
- Kanji 虎 (tiger) repeated "虎虎" as a secondary mark, brand book pp.14, 18, 19.
