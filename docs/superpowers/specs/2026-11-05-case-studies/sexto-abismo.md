## Body (one paragraph = one block)

Sexto Abismo is a gin from San Luis Potosí. The name comes from the Sótano de las Golondrinas in the Huasteca Potosina, one of the deepest abysses on earth. Three partners started it in 2021, José Pablo among them. There was no client and no brief. There was a bottle to fill, and a region most gin drinkers had never heard of.

Most gin looks the same: British, botanical, predictable. Mexico has its own mystique, and Mexican surrealism has more material than one label can hold. The hard part was choosing. The story had to matter as much as the liquid, because the liquid alone would sit quietly on a shelf.

The icon is a swallow taking flight from an abyss under an eclipse: a passage, a change of state. The wordmark is a custom organic letterform with shifting sizes. The label tells a story. A stranger crosses an abyss, meets La Felina and El Lacayo, drinks something clear and wakes in another world. Deep color, luxury detail, a bottle made to be picked up and turned around.

Name and story. Brand book, bottle label and the box of six. Posters, stickers, postcards, a menu, a billboard, a mural, tent cards with the house cocktails, a trailer and a bartender's book. In 2025, Atla wrote a content strategy and a refresh deck for the brand.

The gin shipped and is sold in Mexico. The Beverage Testing Institute gave it a Silver Medal and "Highly Recommended" in 2024.

## We would say this to a CMO

- Origin is the only story a competitor cannot copy.
- Build the world first. The label is one window into it.

### The problem underneath
- From Atla's own case text: "The challenge was to create an identity from scratch to find elements of surrealism that were relevant to the brand … With Mexican surrealism being full of elements, it was really difficult to create storytelling that is as important as the product itself", Drive Textos Portafolio, 2025-01-20.
- Carousel copy: "Most gins feel the same. British. Botanical. Predictable. But Mexico has its own mystique.", Drive Portafolio Carrousels Nuevos, 2025-07-16.
- No brief document from a client exists (JP was the client).

### The idea chosen and why
- Name: "Inspirado en el 'Sexto Abismo' más grande del mundo, El Sótano de las Golondrinas en la Huasteca Potosina", Drive Libro Bartender, 2024-02-13.
- Icon: "el icono se conforma por una golondrina emprendiendo vuelo (El Sótano de las Golondrinas = sexto abismo más grande del mundo) desde un abismo, con una representación gráfica de un eclipse sobre ella. El eclipse simboliza una etapa transitoria, y en antiguos pueblos significaba la manifestación divina.", SneekPeak brand book, 2022-06-03.
- Logotype: "un wordmark en una tipografía orgánica custom … Contiene un juego de tamaños en sus letras para darle dinamismo", same.
- Narrative (label art): "Forastero atraviesa un abismo, encuentra un oasis surrealista con La Felina y el Lacayo … bebe un líquido incoloro que cambia su percepción. Despierta en un nuevo mundo surrealista, la trinidad lo observa orgullosa.", Libro Bartender.
- Double meaning of "abismo": "Tangible, vacío en la tierra sin fondo perceptible. Intangible, realidad inmaterial vasta e insondable.", same.
