## Body (one paragraph = one block)

Two industrial designers in San Pedro Garza García were turning a white warehouse into a place for children and families: art workshops, free play, a library, a shop and a café under one roof. They had a working name, The Lab, and one firm belief: art is a process, not a result. They needed a name and a brand before the space existed.

The hard part was the market, not the logo. Families there are used to structured entertainment and immediate results. The founders wanted to teach the value of free play and of paint on the floor, "where every stain of paint is a win." The identity also had to hold two audiences in one room: children at the tables and parents with a good coffee.

Two directions: Playful Curiosity, geometric shapes that become characters, and Wholesome Imagination, storybook illustration. Laura Díaz de León chose the first: easy to keep in your head. The name came after the direction: Casa Colora, the house of color. A logotype a little disorganized and still balanced, a house-shaped seal, and stickers of clouds, hearts, hands and houses, drawn fat, like clay or shapes cut by small hands. Brown for the type, so beige and stickers never read as babyish.

Naming. Logotype in vertical and horizontal versions, the house seal, a palette of Soft Cream, Vivid Clay, Electric Azure, Dusty Rose, Sky Breeze and Muted Sage, Dunbar Tall with Galano Grotesque, the sticker graphics, photography direction, signage, cups, cards, a calendar and an Instagram system, all in a brand book.

## We would say this to a CMO

- Let the concept come first and the name second. Casa Colora was named by its own graphics.
- Two audiences in one room means one palette doing two jobs.

## Facts

### The situation the client was in
- Brief: "es un espacio integral de creatividad y juego para niños. Representa curiosidad, descubrimiento y libertad de expresión." "Une arte, juego libre, lectura, comunidad, tienda y cafetería en un mismo concepto cuidadosamente diseñado.", Notion brief, 2025.
- Audience: "familias con niños de entre 2 y 10 años … padres jóvenes, principalmente entre 28 y 45 años … nivel socioeconómico medio-alto y alto … San Pedro Garza García", brief.
- "art lab si tenia pero se va a cambiar." Positioning wanted: "creatividad / crear". Values: "creatividad, libertad, desarrollo, confianza, simplicidad". Admired: LEGO, Brio, Cuboro. Not admired: Mattel, Hasbro, KidZania, brief.
- Laura wanted a playground; her partner "quiere darle muchísimo peso a la parte de arte", Laura, Granola 2025-10-31. Founders are industrial designers, not graphic designers (same call, summary).

### The problem underneath
- "El mayor reto … es educar al público sobre el valor del juego libre y del arte como proceso, en un mercado acostumbrado a resultados inmediatos y entretenimiento estructurado.", brief.
- "todo gira en torno al proceso, no al resultado. Las clases de arte no buscan que los niños pinten 'bonito'", brief.
- The identity had to join "un espacio de adultos y de niños, pero al mismo tiempo que sea muy creativo", Adriana, All-Hands 2025-12-04. JP: the café and adult spaces "le da un poquito de balance", same call.
- JP: "el problema con ella es su indecisión", 2025-12-04. Name was still open after kickoff: "Estamos terminando de revisar unas cosas pendientes y el nombre.", Laura, email 2025-11-04.

### The idea chosen and why
- Two directions presented 2025-10-31 by Tais: 1 "Playful Curiosity" (juego, energía, descubrimiento; strong controlled palette with earth tones; geometric shapes that become characters; "descomponer el logo") and 2 "Wholesome Imagination" (calidez, conexión; softer colours; storybook illustration), Gemini notes + Granola.
- Laura: Direction 1 is "fácil de quedarse en la cabeza"; "siento que el uno es más fácil, de entender el concepto, pero el segundo se me hace muy diferente." Daniela: "yo me inclino bastante por la ruta uno, porque creo que puede vivir fácilmente en el mundo digital". JP: "El segundo está increíble. Pero … sí está muy difícil de ejecutarlo.", transcript 2025-10-31.
- Direction 1 was executed. Adriana: "salió superrápido porque el moodboard estaba muy bien definido, y también el concepto de la marca … me encantó", 2025-12-04.
- Logo: "un poco desorganizado, creativo, pero al mismo tiempo tiene cierto equilibrio", Adriana. The house frame "al principio inició siendo un ícono"; JP proposed it as an alternate logo use tied to the sticker graphics, 2025-12-04.
- Brown kept for type over colour after the client doubted it; "fondos beige con los stickers de colores … no se ve tan niño", 2025-12-04.
- Concept line: "a vibrant celebration of our identity: the 'House of Color.'", Brand Guidelines, 2025-12-19.

### What we made (deliverables, named)
- Naming → "Casa Colora" (deck 2025-11-25).
- Logotype, vertical + horizontal variants, small-use version, house-framed seal (packaging, signage, social icon), clearspace, don'ts, Brand Guidelines PDF.
- Palette: Soft Cream #FDFAE2, Vivid Clay #FF413F, Electric Azure #3EBFFF, Dusty Rose #FEC8FC, Sky Breeze #B1E5F2, Muted Sage #B4CE9A, Midnight Anchor #151A1C, Roasted Earth #8B4632. Type: Dunbar Tall (headlines), Galano Grotesque (body), PDF.
- Graphic system: sticker shapes (clouds, hearts, ovals, hands, house) "meant to suggest organic objects made of clay or shapes carefully cut by small hands", PDF. Adri: "están todos como gorditos, las manos están gorditas", 2025-12-04.
- Photography direction, Brand in Action (business card, calendar, signage, cups, posts), Instagram profile/posts/stories, PDF.
