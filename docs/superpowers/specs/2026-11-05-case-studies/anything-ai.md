## Lead (Sanity `description`)

A rename, a wordmark and a launch for the AI app builder that wanted to feel human.

(Replaces: "Brand identity and digital design for an AI-powered creative platform that helps users transform ideas into tangible products and stories.")

## Body (one paragraph = one block)

Create was an AI app builder from San Francisco. You typed what you wanted and it came back as a working product, built with code. The company had been underestimated. Some early users had a bad experience, bigger rivals took the attention, and a launch date was already on the calendar. The brand had to be ready before it.

The brief put the whole problem in one sentence: "We're human in a space dominated by robotic AI branding." The team wanted to feel premium. Powerful first, then beautiful, then intuitive. Not another glow, not another gradient. In the middle of the project they chose a new name for the product, Anything, and the identity had to carry it.

The first route had a symbol. The client saw a news network in it, so we dropped symbols altogether. The brand became typographic: an editorial serif italic set against a plain grotesk, half and half, so the wordmark itself shows an idea turning into a product. A second mark, overlapping shapes tilted upward, became the icon the team calls the rocket. Photography went soft and dreamlike. A computer standing alone in a field.

Logo and symbol. A palette of light blue and deep navy with pink and cream. Type hierarchy, icons, three photography directions, the homepage with its "i want to build a…" prompt, social templates, launch assets, and a brand book in PDF and Figma. Later, animations for the logo, the symbol and the icons.

Zaria Zinn, Founding Designer, the day after launch: "our launch was yesterday and it went very well!! Thank you all so so much for your help getting us ready for it! Also wanted to say the computer in a field image has been a huge hit! Everyone can't stop talking about it!" The funding round that followed made TechCrunch.

## We would say this to a CMO

- When every brand in the category glows, type is the fastest way to look like nobody else.
- A rename mid-project is not a setback. It is the brief getting clearer.
- The image people repeat is the brand. Make one worth repeating.

## Facts

### The situation the client came with
- Inbound call 2025-04-21; proposal same day; Zaria paused it 2025-05-12, returned 2025-06-13 ("We are interested in moving forward")., Gmail 1965a83f7ef46187.
- Tally brief, Zaria, 2025-06-17: "Our current name is 'Create.' We're exploring a rename but haven't committed yet." "We do (logo, name, colors, fonts) but we're open to changing everything. Nothing is off the table."
- "We're not yet the leader. We've been underestimated, and some users had a negative experience with our early product. But we're rebuilding."
- "We currently lack dominant market share and visibility. Many users choose competitors, and we don't always stand out. This rebrand is a critical chance to shift perception and lead."
- Audience: "Non-technical founders, solopreneurs, indie hackers, designers, marketers, and some developers." Competitors: "Bolt, Lovable, Replit, Agent, V0."
- Pressure: "we have a big launch going out (aiming for July 7th) and need to lock in this new branding asap.", Zaria, 2025-06-25.

### The problem underneath
- "We're human in a space dominated by robotic AI branding.", Tally, 2025-06-17.
- Misconception to beat: "the idea that our product isn't as powerful or as beautiful as the other competitors." Priority: "Powerful, then beautiful, then intuitive." Colors: "Something more premium… everything feels really elevated.", Zaria, 2025-06-20.
- "We want to make sure we stand out in the vibe coding scene… feel unique, elevated, almost unexpected… not just in line with everyone else in the space. One idea: exploring a serif font.", Zaria, 2025-07-08.
- Daniela, internal 2025-07-22: client "no quería que se sienta tan digital, ajeno… o tan robótico"; the cue was "el cielo, que se sienta más humano… sin límites… dreaming."

### The idea chosen and why
- Moodboard 2025-06-24 (two routes): client picked "the Creative Engine path" (Zaria), "slightly more edgy" (Marcus).
- V1 2025-07-01 (eye symbol, orange/blue, 2-day sprint) rejected 2025-07-02: "The eye symbol in the logo reminds us a bit of CBS News… The orange/blue combination isn't quite landing for us. However, we like the rich blues and photography-inspired elements." Atla replied recommending keeping "Create" (2025-07-03).
- 2025-07-10, Zaria: "We are moving our domain to createanything.com and changing the name of the builder product to Anything!! The company will remain Create." JP: "the new name, which I love btw."
- Pivot (Daniela, 2025-07-22): "olvidémonos de los símbolos"; "una marca solo tipográfica, donde la misma tipografía puede tener vida… funcionar como máscara de capa"; serif + sans "una marca mucho más verbal"; blurred AI photos "apelar a la imaginación… este universo fantasioso"; layout reference: Pokémon pages; a second symbol route shown deliberately for contrast ("Yo sabía que iba a elegir la ruta uno").
- V2 2025-07-18 (two routes, Loom). Zaria 2025-07-19: "We're excited about concept 1… I really love the 1/2 and 1/2 logo. Wondering if the sans-serif font is too thin?… We are huge fans of the AI imagery- especially the computer in the field." JP, internal 2025-07-22: client wanted a more masculine read for its core gamer audience.
- V3 2025-07-31: "adjusted the logo and the symbol, giving them more angle so they communicate better with each other." Client chose "this version of the logo… (Just rotated the rocket slightly)" (2025-08-08) and "Let's go with blue!" (2025-08-13).

### What we made (deliverables, named)
- Logo: typographic, Editorial New Italic + Neue Haas Grotesk, customized: "a custom design that shows our creative process… from the first idea to the final product.", Brand Book 2025-08-19.
- Symbol: "an abstract design that shows the creative process. The overlapping shapes symbolize the different layers that come together to create something unique, reflecting our motto 'Anything'. This symbol, which seems to rise, shows the idea that creativity can take off and become real." 24° tilt. Client calls it "the rocket".
- Palette (book): Light Blue #9AC2DB, Dark Blue #022147, Pink #FFBEBF, Cream #FFF5E0.
- Type hierarchy, iconography, three photo directions (Lifestyle / AI-generated / Abstract), applications (homepage with "i want to build a…" prompt), social ("Built with your words"), mockups, Brand Guidelines PDF, Figma brand book, Drive asset folders., Final Assets 2025-08-20.
- Web scope asked 2025-07-21: homepage, product pages, in-product loading animation, social avatars/headers, blog visuals.
- Case-study animations (Logo, Symbol, Icons, SocialPosts mp4), Feb 2026.
- Team: Daniela Barrio (Art Director, concept), Adriana Méndez and Mariela Alata (PM), Paola Díaz, JP; later Tais, José Aceves, "Sebas" (animation).
