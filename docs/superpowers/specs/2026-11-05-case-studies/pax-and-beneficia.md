## Body (one paragraph = one block)

Pax & Beneficia opened in Las Colinas in 2019 and grew across Dallas-Fort Worth, roasting its own beans. The name means Peace and Blessings, and the cafés are built on it: a guest is an honored visitor. The company sells roasted coffee and handcrafted syrups online as well as over the counter. A brand existed. It had not caught up with the mission.

Specialty coffee is crowded and most of it looks alike. The challenge was an identity bold enough to stand out on a shelf and still true to the cultural roots that started the company, a café culture carried from Turkey and Andalusia to Texas. And it had to be a system, because new locations kept coming.

The mark is a tree: deep roots, growth outward. Apercu Mono as the type system, warm earth tones with brighter accents. Then the idea that makes it theirs. The company lives by five values, each a pair of P and B words: Prosper & Bond, Persevere & Become, Pause & Breathe, Patience & Belief, Pursue & Beckon. We turned each one into a linocut illustration, so the values travel on the bag instead of staying in a document.

Logo and logo animation, icon, iconography, pattern and font system. Packaging: coffee bag, box, labels and syrup bottle labels. The five value illustrations, then applied across cups, totes, cans and shop windows.

## We would say this to a CMO

- Values are not a page in the brand book. Draw them and they go on the bag.
- Ask what the name means before you design the mark.
