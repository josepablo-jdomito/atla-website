## Lead (Sanity `description`)

A heritage leather house from Upstate New York, rebuilt for the way it sells today.

(Kept. Sourced: founded 1949 in Endicott, NY; online only, mostly on phones.)

## Body (one paragraph = one block)

Hanks has made full-grain leather belts in Endicott, New York since 1949. Made in the USA, with a guarantee measured in generations. The business sells online only, most of it on a phone. Almost every customer was a man. The company wanted to be known for women's accessories too, and the old identity, an uppercase HANKS inside a Circle-H, had nothing to say to them.

Mia Calleo, who runs Hanks, asked for a light touch: keep the name written out, change the font, keep the H. The question underneath was harder. How does a brand built for one audience make room for a second without losing the first? Our first round leaned western and the client said so: too much cowboy, not enough accessory. Men's and women's goods needed one house with two doors.

We kept the structure of the wordmark and reset it in a serif, Proxima Sera, with a monogram H marked EST 1949. The work that mattered was the palette. A saddle-brown core for everything Hanks, then two secondary families: Tanned Oak and Raw Umber for men's pieces, Forged Teal and Washed Patina for women's. Photography moved away from the ranch. The client fixed the tagline herself: crafted in America, not just designed here.

Brand guidelines: tone of voice, mission, values, the logo system, color, type, photography and social templates. A logo package in five colorways. Website design in Figma for home, category and product pages, phone first, handed to their in-house developer to build.

Mia asked for the book to feel warmer and more welcoming than "male centric", and it was revised the same day. Zach Duncan, the marketing partner who brought Hanks to us, said it plainly: "the brand redesign, I'm very happy with that on the hanks. You guys did a good job."

## We would say this to a CMO

- A palette can do what a logo can't: open a second audience without reprinting the first.
- Listen when the client corrects a claim. "Crafted in America" is a truer sentence than the one we wrote.
- Design the handoff as carefully as the identity. The site is where the brand gets judged.

## Facts

### The situation the client was in
- "Hank was the founder of the business back in 1949" (Mia, Tally, 2025-02-21).
- "Currently 90% male audience": blue-collar, white-collar/retired, CCW users; "Women: We are just starting to expand on this audience" (Tally).
- "I would say we are a leader in the mens leather belts market." "online presence only." (Tally)
- Old identity: "all uppercase word 'HANKS' dominated by what we call the 'Circle-H'… The font used in the logo is Oxtail OT." Old palette "intentionally stark" (Carbon, Buck, Rum, Forest). (Tally)
- Objective: "we are trying to transition to not only being a male brand but being known for carrying both gender accessories. Hopes of developing more women's accessories (belts, wallets, bags)." Positioning wish: "Levis - timeless". (Tally)
- Ask (Mia, 2025-02-18): strategy "not my primary concern"; "primary logo - we do like our Hanks Leather Goods written out, maybe we can just change the font"; icon H "change this a bit but nothing drastic"; type, palette, photography; homepage, product, category pages; "mobile is our biggest concern as ~80% of our customers use mobile. We have an in-house developer".
- Before yes: "We feel the project investment slides were a bit vague and we would like more specifics as to what deliverables we can expect" (Mia, 2025-02-18). Yes after the Feb 19 call (JP, 2025-02-19).

### The problem underneath
- JP to Bosco (Granola 2025-08-20): "tenían una identidad muy fea, la verdad, y como que muy antigua… hicieron el cambio de que solo tenían cosas para hombres, a que la marca funcionara para hombres y mujeres." "muchas cosas estaban hechas en Estados Unidos, pero al mismo tiempo estaban empezando a incorporar materiales italianos." "Ellos querían como que mantener mucho la esencia que tenía antes la marca… ahí peleamos nosotros como para modernizarla lo más posible."
- Round-1 pushback: images "too Western and lacked accessory representation" (Read.ai 2025-03-04); "concerns about the Western theme potentially alienating some customers" (Read.ai 2025-03-18).
- Avoid: "Bright colors, reds, oranges, pinks, purples"; wants "gender neutral colors… whites, blues, greens" (Tally).

### The idea chosen and why
- JP (2025-08-20): "parte del trabajo importante fue llegar mucho a esta paleta de colores, que permitiera que la marca pasara de ser totalmente masculina, a tener cosas que pudieran ser muy útiles para sus colecciones femeninas."
- JP's direction after feedback (to Aceves, 2025-03-18): "fortalecer el sistema de paleta de color, teniendo a lo mejor 3-4 colores principales y algunos colores secundarios que se dividían en dos: artículos de hombres y artículos de mujeres." Logo: "Diferentes acomodos y agreguemos un monograma. Dejar misma tipografía." Photos "menos western".
- Aceves (2025-03-19): "usar el light brown para hombres y el casi turquesa para mujeres, y dejar como color principal un color leather más general… un café rojizo oscuro." JP kept a light blue because "le gustó mucho a la clienta ese color."
- Client on round 2 (via Adriana, 2025-03-20): "en general le gustó mucho y está más alineado a lo que busca… no les gusta mucho la tipografía de HANKS (la K específicamente)". Refined type sent 03-20; approved 03-21: "Al cliente le gustó tu propuesta final (estaba genial!!!)".
- Tagline correction (Mia via Adriana, 2025-03-21): "We love the 'designed in america - consumed by the world' but we want to make sure people know that we dont just design it here and have it made out of the country. They are crafted in america. Additionally, if we were to say consumed by the world people might think we ship worldwide but we do not at this time… besides that, we approve it!"

### What we made (deliverables, named)
- Moodboards (2025-03-04), brand proposal v1 (03-14), revised proposal + mockups (03-19/20).
- **Brand Guidelines 2025** PDF: Tone of Voice, Mission, Vision, Values, Logo (full logo, wordmark, brand mark "H" + "EST 1949"), Color, Typography, Photography, Social Media, Brand Application. Delivered 03-28; revised same day after Mia: "The pages up to 20 were giving me a very male centric feel and darker than I would like… I want it to feel a bit more welcoming and warm… The color usage on page 29 looks so nice!"
- Palette: primary Buffed Hide #F2E7D3, Aged Saddle #743611, Burnished Espresso #281E15; secondary "categorizes products for men and women": Forged Teal #2A4E59, Washed Patina #B2D1DB, Tanned Oak #A4652B, Raw Umber #F1BA87. Type: Proxima Sera, Specter. (guidelines)
- Logo package (5 colorways), fonts, social templates (Drive, Mar 31–Apr 25).
- Website UI in Figma: homepage, product, category, mobile + desktop. Mia (Slack, 2025-04-17): "The updates to the website mocks look great. The only thing I noticed was the left-margin of the logo." Built by their in-house developer.

### What happened after
- Zach Duncan (Granola, 2025-06-12): "the brand redesign, I'm very happy with that on the hanks. You guys did a good job." Also: "it's still a work in progress, it's not finalized what they're doing now"; their "ADA guy" is adding "outlines on everything".
- JP, same call: "I think we did great. Sad to see how they are implementing it, but anyway, I think it looks great."
- JP to Bosco (2025-08-20): "el diseño de la marca… fue bueno, pero luego ellos ya la ejecución web la hicieron ellos y no fue tan buena."
- hanksbelts.com (2026-11-05): "LEATHER THAT LIVES WITH YOU. Full Grain. No Compromises. Made in the USA." Men's and women's menus. No public statement on the rebrand, no public numbers.
