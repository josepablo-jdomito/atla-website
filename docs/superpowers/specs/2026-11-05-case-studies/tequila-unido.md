## Body (one paragraph = one block)

Carlos Alvarado built Tequila Unido alone. Born in Eagle Pass, Texas, grandparents from Guadalajara, father a mariachi singer: "soy tejano jalisciense." He registered the name, partnered with a family distillery in Arandas founded in 1915, carried samples by bus to Dallas distributors, and arrived with a symbol, a bottle and a blue label. The first cases were due in Texas for Mexican Heritage Month. The brand had to be clarified, not replaced.

Look at a tequila shelf and the blue is everywhere. His symbol, a rigid geometric knot in a circle, read like a keyboard key. And Carlos had two conditions. His culture was not a costume. And the story could not be about him: "hay que hacer mi historia en la historia de todos." Unido had to mean something you could say at a bar.

The idea was already in his brief: "unido is how you order it." Good tequila is offered, not drunk alone. We took the mariachi, his father's trade, as the cultural key without naming the family: a medallion label shaped by the sombrero, ornaments from the brooches of a mariachi jacket, a symbol that reads as rope, ribbon or bow. Paper, deep red, gold and green. No blue. The eagle redrawn, because the first one, in his words, was a pigeon.

Moodboard and brand direction. Labels for Blanco, Reposado, Añejo and Extra Añejo, and the small bottle. Brand guidelines with the custom wordmark, symbol, badges, Schoolbook and Latino Gothic, photography direction and the Instagram system. A coming-soon page. The six-pack box and the shipper art, print-ready for the distributor.

Carlos, on the first presentation: "sí me gusta mucho el concepto del sombrero del mariachi con el logo, siento que sí me entendieron." On the final white Blanco label: "I love it. I think that's the one." And: "I feel extremely blessed and happy to have you guys on board."

## We would say this to a CMO

- The best line in the brief is usually the client's. Build the brand on it.
- Heritage is a key, not a costume. Use one object from it and leave the rest.
- Walk the shelf before you pick a color.

## Facts

### The situation the client was in
- Carlos built the brand alone since 2019: registered IP, visited ~15 distilleries, partnered with a 1915 Arandas distillery, carried samples by bus 17 h to Dallas distributors, "me tomó 5 años en sacar la marca", Granola kickoff transcript, 2025-07-29.
- Born in Eagle Pass TX, grandparents from Guadalajara, father a mariachi singer: "soy tejano jalisciense", kickoff, 2025-07-29.
- Already had a name, a registered symmetric "symbol of unity" logo, a bottle with charro-pant engraving, a blue label that met TTB/TABC rules, a previous agency ("tuvimos unos desacuerdos"), and a brandbook ("Open Unido-Brandbook.pdf", Drive, 2025-07-23). Brief questionnaire: "Do you have an established brand identity? Yes but it needs to be clarified and improved", Notion brief, 2025-07-23.
- Launch pressure: first 1,000 cases (6-bottle), Texas, September 2025 (Mexican Heritage Month); first-batch labels due 2025-08-28, Granola 2025-08-05, 2025-08-21.
- Brief answers: target "Hispanic Americans"; position "Challenger and Disruptor"; colors "Blue, red, green, white, the colors of the Mexican and American flags"; visual elements "An eagle, My logo, Moments of unity"; admired brand "Absolute vodka"; not admired "Casa amigos, 818"; competitors "Lalo, Don Julio, Casa amigos, Casa dragones"; long-term goal "Become the preferred tequila of Hispanic Americans"; why choose it: "It's good tequila and unido is how you order it", Notion brief, 2025-07-23.
- Carlos on moodboards: "I need something that looks good everywhere. At a club or at a cookout", Gmail, 2025-07-31.

### The problem underneath
- Category sameness: "en la categoría, cuando vemos toda la competencia, el color azul es tan azul... no queremos ser uno más del montón", Atla, Granola 2025-08-21. The old wordmark was soft and the symbol resembled the Apple Command key (Granola summary 2025-08-21; Brand Proposal p.13: previous symbol "más rígida, geométrica y encapsulada dentro de un círculo").
- Cultural authenticity vs. costume: "mi cultura no es un disfraz... las personas no más le sacan el jugo a la cultura mexicana" and competitors "están como feminizando la cultura", Carlos, kickoff 2025-07-29.
- Story must not be about him: "yo no quiero que la marca se trate sobre mí o de mi familia... es tequila de Carlos Alvarado y su familia... hay que hacer mi historia en la historia de todos", Carlos, kickoff 2025-07-29.
- Label is a ritual, not just a label: "me das un tequila unido, significa juntos, ¿cuántos vas a querer?", Carlos, kickoff 2025-07-29.

### The idea chosen and why
- "Unido" as philosophy and gesture: "good tequila isn't enjoyed alone; it's offered... This is what a Unido moment feels like", Brand Proposal p.3, 2025-08-21.
- Mariachi as the cultural key (his father's trade) without naming the family: medallion label "inspirado en el sombrero del mariachi", ornamental brooches from mariachi jacket closures, symbol readable as rope, ribbon or mariachi bow, Granola 2025-08-21; Proposal p.31.
- Symbol evolved from rigid geometric circle to a calligraphic, organic stroke "cobra vida propia", Proposal p.13.
- Palette: Paper #F7F1E8, Red #721D13, Gold #C59838, Green #374129, Ink #0A0807; blue avoided for shelf differentiation; Carlos asked for a darker green, Brand Guidelines 2025-09-25; Granola 2025-08-21.
- Label option 1 (medallion, embossed, foil) chosen over option 2 (traditional rectangle) as "más arriesgado y más disruptivo en la categoría", Granola 2025-08-21.
- Final Blanco: cream/white label, blue lettering, silver symbol in the coin; one label per variety (Brian: two labels "you'll start to confuse people"); Ethan (sales): "I always see the blue labels... when I see a white label... that's catching my eye", Granola 2025-10-15.
- Eagle redrawn: Carlos: "We need an eagle, not a bird, please. That's a pigeon" (2025-10-15); eagle meaning: Eagle Pass TX + Las Águilas, Guadalajara, "knows no borders" (2025-10-22).
- Extra Añejo in black, rest of family in white; six-pack in red/blue or red/purple; individual boxes dropped for cost, Granola 2025-10-22.
