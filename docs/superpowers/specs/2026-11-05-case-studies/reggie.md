## Body (one paragraph = one block)

Reggie makes calming chews, supplements and feeder mats for dogs. The name is short for regimen. New owners from New Zealand bought it in 2025 and inherited three brands at once: the original, a refresh from the year before, and the watered-down version the old team had actually shipped, white jars and all. They wanted to rebuild the plane while flying it, from direct sales into retail, with a trade show on the calendar.

Simon Doig, co-owner, wrote the problem down: "if we are going to make the change again, we really want to get it right, and produce something special." The refresh looked cleaner and might not stand out on a shelf. The brief banned anything overbearing or clinical. The tone had to be the non-judgmental friend, not the vet with a prescription. Supplements that feel like treats, not medicine.

The idea in our insight work: trust as an emotion. Be the brand that feels like a relationship, not a transaction. Routine as ritual. Science in a human voice. With the partner studio Vapor and the owners' direction: bring the logo out stronger, anchor on the brand green and drop the brown, and give the mascot fill, shading and bolder lines instead of a sketch, so Reggie the dog becomes an asset again. New Kansas with Satoshi. Soft ivory, mid green, a teal dark.

Discovery and insight decks with a three-way comparison of the brands. Brand identity and digital expression: wordmark, logo, type, palettes, photography direction and avatar. Website design: homepage, collection, product, cart and checkout, about, blog, email, pop-ups and a design system. Packaging and labels for the focus products: jars, pouch, dropper and the feeder-mat pack.

Ricardo Camargo, Vapor Studio, when the site went live: "Reggie.com is live and it looks great. Congratulations to the team, great work." The brand was relaunched at SuperZoo in Las Vegas that August.

## We would say this to a CMO

- Three versions of a brand in market is worse than one bad one. Decide, then ship everything at once.
- A mascot is an asset only if you draw it like one.
- Get the publication rights in writing from the owner, not the project lead.

## Facts

### The situation the client was in
- "The brand had a refresh mid 2024 / New Logo, New Colours, New Packaging / The brand is trading between old and new", owners' Branding Deck, Apr 2025.
- "we support the upgrade of the brand, but not the current execution from the current management team!" … "retained the white plastic jars (due to COGS), 100k of white jars in stock but seem to have watered down the new brand", Branding Deck, Apr 2025.
- "100% DTC, has 250k database, with 90k active … Amazon … now represents 20%", intake, 2025-04-11.
- Goal: "evaluate the old brand vs. new brand vs. current in-between brand to then establish a clear direction for the Reggie 2.0 brand across website, product, packaging, and socials", intake.
- "To 'rebuild the brand /plane whilst flying it'"; long-term "a global pet behavioural brand … attracting interest from the private equity markets", intake.
- Ricardo on the 2024 rebrand: "more bold, but there was definitely some issues and readability"; "This is the last rebrand", Granola 2025-06-20.
- Deadlines: SuperZoo Aug 13–15 2025 ("the biggest US pet trade show"); retail Q1/Q2 2026; owners' "brand transition milestone of August 25" (Simon, 2025-05-23).

### The problem underneath
- "we are facing a scenario of old brand, 2024 brand update (product labels), and now a 2025 new brand look / refresh … if we are going to make the change again, we really want to get it right, and produce something special.", Simon Doig, 2025-05-23.
- "The new brand and packaging concepts look cleaner, but we question if they will stand out enough within a competitive landscape", Simon, 2025-05-23. Yet the brief bans "Anything overbearing, in your face, rigid"; tone "Approachable expert", "Your non-judgemental friend / guide".
- DTC → retail: "become a trusted brand that they recognize … multiple SKUs … multiple form factors" (big/small jar, stand-up pouch, lick mats), Ricardo, 2025-06-20.
- "Reggie treats and reggie supplements rather than being medical … It isn't like, hey. You need to take your medicine", Ricardo, 2025-06-20.
- "Currently not winning, but its focus on calming has contributed to its revenue.", intake.
- JP's Insight Deck: Zesty Paws "Generic, lacks emotional story"; Finn "Needs scientific depth"; Native Pet "Clinical, low warmth"; Reggie "Needs refresh to scale & resonate".

### The idea chosen and why
- JP, Insight Deck 2025-04-22: "Trust as an Emotion: Be the brand that feels like a relationship, not a transaction"; "Routine = Ritual"; "Smart Science, Human Tone"; "Reggie isn't just in the pet business / Reggie is in the human connection business"; "Let's build the first pet brand that talks to humans like we talk to our dogs. Loyal. Real. Unfiltered."
- Phase 1: "They really liked everything. Great work." … "I like pushing them into new territory.", Ricardo, 2025-05-23.
- Owner direction: "the name Reggie has the ability to form a really bold brand, so really want to bring the brand logo out even stronger"; "investigate more options around mascot, including fill shading, bolder lines as options vs. the current sketch format"; "use of the Reggie 'R' for socials, and embroidery on Feeder Matts"; "Do we think we need a tag line?", Simon, 2025-05-23.
- Colour: "Anchored by the brand GREEN", "eliminating the brown" (Ricardo, 2025-05-22); owners "not sure about … the pink and purples"; "they felt more comfortable with the ivory … Maybe it was too much color", Ricardo, 2025-06-20.
- Naming: "we are ReggiePet" (Reggie Wellness "could literally be human"), Ricardo, 2025-06-20.
- Logo: "refined by removing the thick blue border … better legibility and a more contemporary feel", Brand Manual, 2025-06-27.
- Levi's draft case (2026): "reconcile two visual territories often treated as opposites: scientific credibility and emotional warmth"; "reclaim the mascot as a meaningful brand asset".

### What we made (deliverables, named)
- Phase 1 (May 2025): discovery deck; wordmark/logo direction; mascot sketches; palettes; 3-way packaging comparison (original / 2024 / new).
- Phase 2 (2025-07-24, Adriana): **Brand Identity & Digital Expression** (Figma "Reggie Brand Book", `Reggie_2025.07.24_Brand Book.pdf`); **Website Experience (Design Only)**: Homepage, Collection, Product, Cart Checkout, Design System, About Us, Blog template, Email Marketing, Pop-ups, Subscription Form, Social Avatars.
- Brand book: Wordmark, Logo, Logo Comparison, Typography (**New Kansas** / **Satoshi**), Palette v1 (Soft Ivory #FFEEE6, Mid Green #39A239, Teal Dark #04402E; Mango Sunset, Whisper Lilac, Sky Blue, Light Coral), Palette v2 (Pale Peach, Mango Sunset, Burnt Umber #670016), Photography ("Active & Happy Lifestyle", "Wellness & Care"), Avatar icon.
- **Packaging & Label System (Focused SKUs)**: jar labels (70×80 mm white, 90×90 mm clear), stand-up pouch, dropper, feeder-mat pack; retail templates (Petco end cap / independent / countertop) scoped 2025-06-20; final comments 2025-07-31; Drive folder "Reggie Final adjustments".
