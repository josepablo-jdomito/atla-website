## Body (one paragraph = one block)

Two partners were opening a boutique studio in Abu Dhabi: infrared-heated Pilates, sculpt, barre and mat classes, with hot saunas and cold plunges. The interior was already decided, oak and curves and green. The name was chosen, Arc, for movement, growth and story. There was no identity, and the studio would be the first in the city to offer heated sculpt and mat Pilates.

The founders put the problem in their brief: most Pilates studios "feel extremely similar and lack distinction. They tend to be black-and-white, convenient but not memorable." They wanted a destination, not a timetable. The strategy went further. Arc does not compete with gyms. It competes with everything that asks for your time. The position was recovery first, in a market that sells intensity, which is harder to hold than it sounds.

Two moodboards, Momentum Within and Collected Flow. The direction that carried: a place where intensity and calm coexist. The symbol is cut from the capital A of the wordmark, the curve that remains when the straight side and the bar are removed, a figure in motion. Three gradient families, one per pillar: orange for movement, brown for recovery and the spaces, green for the studio and its community. Area, a quiet typeface, for strength without ornament.

Strategy and moodboards. The graphic path: palette, logo, type, gradients, photography and applications, from posters to a playlist cover. The brand symbol. A brand guidelines book with the verbal identity, the pillars, wordmark and symbol, color, gradients, pillar icons, photography rules, Instagram system and favicon. Final logo files.

## We would say this to a CMO

- When the category sells intensity, calm is a position. It is also the harder one to hold.
- Name the pillars before the colors. Then every gradient has a job.

## Facts

### The situation the client was in
- Pre-launch. "We're building a boutique fitness studio in Abu Dhabi that blends luxury, wellness, and community through infrared-heated Pilates, barre, and mat workouts... In the short term, we aim to open our first location, build brand awareness, and grow a loyal client base.", client brief, 2025-08-24.
- No identity yet: "Do you have an established brand identity? No"; brand colors "We haven't established thats yet"; avoid "very bright colors", brief, 2025-08-24.
- Name and meaning: "My brand is called Arc Studio. It represents movement, growth, and story.", brief, 2025-08-24.
- Interior already decided: "Oak wood furniture / kumo dubai restaurant interior / curved shapes / green / luxurious wellness studio", brief, 2025-08-24. Aceves: green suits them because competitors are "muy blanco y negro o café, o hay uno que es como que muy intenso, como color rojo" and the client's interior is oak, Granola 2025-09-09.
- Competitors named: Kaizen Pilates, BRRN Barre, Form Studio; threat: "brrnbarre is located in Dubai... if they open a studio in Abu Dhabi it could replace our studio", brief. Admired: BodyByBerner, BRRN, Barry's.
- Timeline: Maryam confirmed go 2025-07-17; JP chased 07-25 ("we have reached out multiple times"); Fatima confirmed 07-26 and asked to split payment; questionnaire done and first payment 2025-08-25 ("We've just submitted the initial payment. We're excited to get started !", Fatima). Fatima on 2025-09-08: "We haven't heard back from you in 12 days since starting... We need clarity on the projects current status and next steps." First presentation 2025-09-12; JP opened with "Sorry again for the miscommunication over the the last few weeks", Granola transcript.

### The problem underneath
- Category sameness: "We don't particularly admire most pilates studios, as many of them feel extremely similar and lack distinction. They tend to be black-and-white, convenient but not memorable. With Arc, we want to go beyond convenience, creating a true destination that offers a unique experience people are excited to be part of.", brief, 2025-08-24.
- New concept in a new market: "Our biggest challenges are introducing a new concept to the Abu Dhabi market, and standing out in a competitive fitness and wellness space.", brief.
- First-mover claim: "We will be the first hot bar in Abu Dhabi" (brief); guidelines reframe it as "the first studio in Abu Dhabi to offer heated sculpt and mat Pilates".
- Strategy insight: "Arc doesn't compete with gyms, it competes with everything that asks for time and attention. The emotional driver here is transformation with meaning... It's not about burning calories. It's about belonging.", Brand Guidelines p.009.
- JP, 2026-03-11 newsletter: "The brief was clear: build something that leads with recovery, not performance. In a market saturated with intensity, that's a harder position to hold than it sounds."

### The idea chosen and why
- Two moodboards presented 2025-09-12: "Momentum Within" (holistic, calm intensity, greens + oak, serene minimal photography) and "Collected Flow" (dynamic, community, brighter natural light). Internally JP preferred the first ("me gusta más el primero"), Granola 2025-09-09. Client: "I like them both... I really like the titles of every recovery room has a title, and the active room has a title... I think it brings the brand together." (Fatima, 2025-09-12 transcript). No decision on the call; deck sent via WhatsApp.
- Graphic Path (Proposal 01, 2025-09-26) describes the chosen direction: "a wellness experience that is both grounding and invigorating, a place where intensity and calm coexist in balance... discover strength in the calm momentum of Arc Studio." Logo brief on the deck: "Custom + Luxurious + Approachable". Read.ai 09-26: palette "green, reddish-orange, and warm brown to embody wellness, intensity, and tranquility".
- Symbol: "The Arc symbol... originates from the letter A, yet evolves into something more, a human form in motion." (Guidelines p.026). Brand Symbol PDF 2025-10-03: "a direct extraction from the capital letter 'A' in the logo, specifically using the curved line that remains after removing the straight vertical side and the middle bar... abstractly represents a figure in motion".
- Pillar icons (Movement, Recovery, Community) and three gradient families, each tied to a pillar: orange = movement ("energy, heat, and flow"), brown = recovery and spaces, green = Arc Studio and community, Guidelines p.033.
- Typeface: Area (Blaze Type), Normal + Extended, "quiet strength over ornamental complexity", Guidelines p.035.
