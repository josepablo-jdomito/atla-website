## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Torre Avancer is an office tower on Avenida Chapultepec in San Luis Potosí, one of the holdings of Grupo Avancer, a company with businesses in flavors and fragrances, finance, real estate and hotels. The building came before the brand. Its irregular geometry was drawn to recall the semi-desert around the city, and the tower had already won awards when the owners asked us for its identity.

The hard part, as we wrote in our notes at the time, was working inside identities that already existed, starting with the architecture itself. So the logotype is purely typographic, set in capitals, TORRE in a light weight and AVANCER in bold. The icon is a set of vertical lines taken from the architect's own sketches of the tower. The aim was a brand quiet enough to point at the building's details without covering them.

The logotype and the icon. A signage system drawn for the building: the entrance totem, the lobby directory with its restaurant and bar, pictograms, and a logo plate cut from metal. Stationery, folders and posters in dusty pink, terracotta and rust, with the sketch of the tower printed in ink. Social media for the tower and its tenants from 2021 to 2023.

We made it in 2022 at MK+2, José Pablo's studio before Atla, working with Elipsis Arquitectos. Signage was a service the studio had never offered before. It started with this building, drawn so the signs would read as part of the structure rather than additions to it.
