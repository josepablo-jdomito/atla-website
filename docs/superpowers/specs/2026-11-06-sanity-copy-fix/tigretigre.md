## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
TigreTigre is a sushi and sake restaurant on Avenida Chapultepec in San Luis Potosí, opened by José Pablo and his partners in 2023. The pitch fits on a sticker: after-office sake and food. Japanese comfort plates made with good ingredients, cocktails and premium sake, a place to land when the day ends. The brand was made at MK+2, his studio before Atla, before the doors opened.

The restaurant is casual on a Tuesday, with ramen and sake, and formal on a Saturday, with author plates, and it needed a brand that could be both. The name already repeats itself. The identity had to turn that repetition into a character, so the place would read as one restaurant across both moods.

The wordmark mixes a casual type style with a formal one, so the name moves. The icon is a double-headed tiger sharing one eye, the duality and the repetition taken straight from the name. A kanji mark, 虎虎, covers the small places. Stickers carry the voice for everything else, with lines like "Sake it off" and "Have a rice day."

The logotype and its variants, the double-headed tiger, the kanji mark, color and type. Stationery and cards, stickers and labels, the menu, posters and flyers, a bag and packaging mockup, social templates and the design of the first website.

The restaurant is open, with a new menu for 2026.
