## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Ando is a berry-flavored gummy made to prevent and ease alcohol flush, also known as Asian flush. The company is in Brooklyn. Its founders, Peter Czepiga and Ray Margono, met as college freshmen. Ray is Indonesian and had lived with the flush his whole adult life. In 2023, before their first production run, they came to MK+2, José Pablo's studio before Atla, for a brand that made health feel joyful.

The name was still being tested when we started, and in production the gummy itself turned a dark red, between raspberry and red wine. We chose to keep the brand free of the product's color. José Pablo put it to the founders directly: "I don't believe that the brand must be aligned to the gummy color." Crimson stayed in the palette as an accent beside cobalt and mint.

The wordmark is heavy and rounded, black on cream. The pouch explains the product in plain steps, starting with taking Ando before you drink, and leads with a promise you can say out loud: less flush, more fun. Billboards and ads carry the same voice, built around the joy of going out without the flush.

Visual identity, color, the packaging dieline, home and product page designs in Figma, and a brand book. In 2024, revised labels and a travel tin, which Peter briefed as "Fun/playful/joyful, like everything that comes from our brand."

On the home page design, Peter wrote: "Ray and I LOVE this design! I think your team did an amazing job." Ando is on sale today at andogummy.com, and new clients have come to Atla since after seeing the project.
