## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Create was an AI app builder from San Francisco. You typed what you wanted and it came back as a working product, built with code. The company had been underestimated. Some early users had a bad experience, bigger rivals took the attention, and a launch date was already on the calendar. The brand had to be ready before it.

The brief put the whole problem in one sentence: "We're human in a space dominated by robotic AI branding." The team wanted to feel premium: powerful first, then beautiful, then intuitive, and free of the glow and gradients the category leans on. In the middle of the project they chose a new name for the product, Anything, and the identity had to carry it.

The first route had a symbol. The client saw a news network in it, so we dropped symbols altogether. The brand became typographic: an editorial serif italic set against a plain grotesk, so the wordmark itself shows an idea turning into a product. A second mark, overlapping shapes tilted upward, became the icon the team calls the rocket. Photography went soft and dreamlike, with a computer standing alone in a field.

The logo and symbol. A palette of light blue and deep navy with pink and cream. A type hierarchy, icons, photography directions, the homepage with its "i want to build a..." prompt, social templates, launch assets, and a brand book in PDF and Figma. Later, animations for the logo, the symbol and the icons.

Zaria Zinn, founding designer, the day after launch: "our launch was yesterday and it went very well!! Thank you all so so much for your help getting us ready for it! Also wanted to say the computer in a field image has been a huge hit! Everyone can't stop talking about it!" The funding round that followed made TechCrunch.
