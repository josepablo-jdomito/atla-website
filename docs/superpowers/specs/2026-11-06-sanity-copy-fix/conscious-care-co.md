## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
We built this identity for Conscious Care Co at MK+2, José Pablo's studio before Atla, in early 2024. The design is made for kits of care at home: prompt cards for families, a candle, a book and a tote, sold from a site that invites you to explore the kits and nurture your home.

The system has a monogram, a primary logo and aligned versions, revised at the client's request to add a flower and a pair of hands. Dark green, brown and cream set the ground, with orange, light green, lavender and purple on top. VJ Category sets the titles and Mulish the text. The voice is direct: care about you.
