## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
A mezcal producer from San Luis Potosí came to us with a brand called Xantolo and asked for a complete renewal. The product was good. The brand looked childish and cheap, dressed in the Day of the Dead the way a movie would dress it. He wanted something that could compete in a demanding market with a daring presence, and keep the elegance of how mezcal is made.

The first decision was the name. Anything tied to the Day of the Dead or a national holiday would keep the brand inside the costume. The verbal identity had to carry courage, daring and pride, so the name became Alma Brava, brave soul: the part of you that fights for what it wants without fear of the limits along the way.

The identity is black, almost nothing else, with a typeface of generous proportions and a seal. It is elegant and fine, so the daring lives in the name and the restraint lives in the object. The brand book carries the line that sums it up: "Sentimiento icónico, orgullo de nuestra tierra."

The naming and the verbal identity. A logotype, a monogram and a seal. The brand book. The front and back label and the art direction for the bottle, and a product photoshoot. We made it at MK+2, José Pablo's studio before Atla, between 2020 and 2021, with José Pablo as art director and Andrés Domínguez as creative director.

The Dieline and Packaging of the World both selected the work in 2021. The name was registered as a trademark the following year.
