## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Fernando Ramirez started Angeles after a long stretch of stomach pain no doctor could explain. Herbal cleansing protocols worked where the visits had not. The brand grew fast, burned him out, and lost its customers when he renamed it away from an extreme niche. He came to Atla with a large audience that no longer answered, and one ask: "It isn't performing well. I know it could be doing way better."

The product is a complete herbal system in phases, clear and then nourish, priced above the shelf. The problem was translation. The site had to teach what the products do so that anyone could follow it. Fernando's words: "Dumb it down." It also had to avoid looking cheap or pushy. Buyers of wellness, the strategy found, are tired of being sold to and tired of fake wellness.

The character we gave the brand is a health strategist: confident, direct and calm, a man who has seen both sides. The line that carried was your body isn't broken, it's blocked. Fernando picked the quietest of our moodboards, with the type and imagery of another. The logo is Magalie, softened and customized. Gradients follow the sky from sunrise to sunset, one for each product, the change a body goes through on its way back.

A moodboard and a strategy deck. Audience validation with personas. The graphic path: logo, a palette in purple, blue and orange, Playfair Display with ITC Avant Garde Gothic, jar boxes, social templates, a tote, cards and the website hero. Brand guidelines. Packaging for capsules, electrolytes and loose-leaf tea, with labels and box dielines. Event pieces: a banner, a hat, a booth and business cards.

Fernando, at the graphic path presentation: "honestly, I'm impressed right now. I have a smile that I can't hold back." Later, in writing: "I decided to give you all full reins on the rebrand." On the tea packs: "The doypack for the teas look fantastic." His site now opens with the strategy line: "Your body isn't broken. It's backed up."
