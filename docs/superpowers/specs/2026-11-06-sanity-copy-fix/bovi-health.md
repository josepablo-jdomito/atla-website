## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Bovi Health sells grass-fed beef colostrum and beef organ capsules. Its founders are Shannon Berg, a former college athlete, and Colson Myers, who has trained all his life, and their own line for the company is that health should be simple. They came to Atla at the end of 2024 with products made in the USA by a contract manufacturer and asked for the brand, the packaging and a site to sell from.

We showed them a rustic route with a community feel and another that mixed health science with luxury. Colson picked the rustic one and moved it east: rolling pastures, flowers, denim, red barns, embroidery, bottled milk and cows, "more 'east-coast farm' vibe as opposed to the more western vibe." Shannon fixed the words on the seal herself, Nature's Gold on top and Made in the USA below.

The wordmark is a cursive script drawn by hand, set in a roundel with a cow etched in a vintage line. The palette is soft: cream, sky blue, blush pink and a deep brown. Neutra Text and Nautica carry the type. The photography brief asks for love of the homeland and of the animals, and the pouches are shown in open pasture.

Vibe boards and moodboards, the logo and the brand guidelines, and labels for both products, taken through the manufacturer's compliance review. Then the website, which grew from a landing page into a full Shopify store with bundles and a page on why Bovi.

The manufacturer's quality lead wrote that the labels gave him "a farmhouse chic vibe that I think will do very well with consumers." At handover in May 2025, Shannon wrote: "It all looks great... We are very excited about how this turned out."
