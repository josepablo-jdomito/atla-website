## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Castro Capital invests in real estate development in San Luis Potosí. Its co-founders, Moisés and Sebastián Castro Martín del Campo, wanted the firm to be an ally to clients building commercial property, someone to help them make the best choices along the way. In 2021 they asked MK+2, José Pablo's studio before Atla, for an identity to match that ambition.

The brief came down to a balance. The firm wanted to be seen as powerful and respected, and also as close and trustworthy. The monogram answers it by joining the C of Castro and the C of Capital with a plus sign, for what the firm adds to its projects and its investors. Around it, a modern and minimal system on a geometric grid, where the R and the P of the wordmark step forward.

Navy and royal blue carry the weight, with orange and cream for warmth, on a brand book, letterhead, email signatures, business cards and social templates. The posters speak to investors in plain Spanish: high-impact development, with transparent and concrete plans for where the money goes. The final files went out at the end of 2021.

Mindsparkle Mag published the work in March 2022. Through the following year, MK+2 sent it to prospective clients as a sample of its work.
