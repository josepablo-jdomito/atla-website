## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Oxylife is oxygenated water in a can, produced in Prague and imported into the United Arab Emirates. Suhail Alameri brought it to MK+2, José Pablo's studio before Atla, in 2021. He described it as the most stable oxygenated water in the world, sealed in a patented can coated on the inside. When we started, the brand still had no settled name.

The identity is a thin, wide serif wordmark on a white can, with black and a cool cyan. The label works in English and in Arabic. It carries the product's claim, unique oxygenated water, the line Power for your life, a heart drawn from the water and oxygen formulas, and small icons for when to drink it: driving, flying, at the gym, at work.

A branding presentation, the logo and the can label. MK+2 used the work as one of its sales cases through the following year, and the water is still produced in Prague today.
