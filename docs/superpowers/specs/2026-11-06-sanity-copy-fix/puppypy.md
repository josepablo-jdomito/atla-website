## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
PuppyPy makes silicone holders that clip a tracking tag onto a dog's collar. In 2022 its founder, Gilad Rom, was renaming and rebuilding the company, then called Huan, to come back to market with a product that had already proved itself. MK+2, José Pablo's studio before Atla, took it on that spring, as the studio was starting to work with clients in the United States.

We started with the name. Gilad picked Puppy Py, after his own dog, Puppy Pie, whose spelling was already taken, and asked us: "If you can find a creative way around that it would be amazing!" The answer runs the name together in lowercase, stacked as pu ppy py, with a pPy monogram for the small places.

Color, illustration and photography carry the rest: bright yellow, red, orange, pink and lime, a dachshund wearing a rainbow holder, and a line that sums up the product, stand out from the pack. The moodboard, the branding presentation and the final files went out in June 2022. Gilad then redesigned the website himself on top of them.

He also introduced us to a friend who, in Gilad's words, "was very impressed with the work you did for me!"
