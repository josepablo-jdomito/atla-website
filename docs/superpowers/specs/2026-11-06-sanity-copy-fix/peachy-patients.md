## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Peachy is a healthcare startup built for patients, founded by Lex Oiler, a designer who became a founder. In 2022 the team came to MK+2, José Pablo's studio before Atla, after trying to brand the company themselves. Lex put the problem in one sentence: "I had no idea branding for my startup would end up being the hardest part of my job."

The wordmark is a heavy serif, customized so every letter fits its neighbors while keeping its own bars and curves. To balance that weight, the icon is a smiling heart with a leaf on top, light and friendly, a little like a hairstyle. The team chose the bolder version of the logotype. Stickers carry the voice: for patients by patients, healthcare made easy, along for the ride with you.

The branding presentation, the logotype and the icon, a palette of peach, red, pink, light blue, yellow and cream, a set of stickers, and applications from posters and banners to envelopes and cards. The elements and assets went out in June 2022. That summer we also designed a quick-pay screen for the product, so patients could pay their providers faster.

Lex's verdict, written for us to share: "My only regret was not finding José and his team sooner. I'd recommend them to any of my friends." The following year he sent us Pathize Health.
