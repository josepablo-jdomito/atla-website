## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Baristio is a coffee company built around the person behind the bar. Its app lets you choose your barista before you choose your drink, and the business planned to start on delivery apps, with a shop to follow. Emilio Orozco, a co-founder, came to us in 2025, before anything had launched. The contract put the goal in one line: a solid brand from day one.

Our first proposal read too masculine to Emilio. He wanted the brand to welcome women as much as men, and he pointed us to Good News Coffee in Barcelona. He also worried that a thin-line mark would break down in print and as an app icon. We reworked the direction after that meeting, and he approved it at the end of July.

The idea we wrote down was craft with people at the center: the barista promoted from server to maker, the customer from a sale to a relationship. The wordmark is custom, a condensed serif with sharp contrast, and a ©B monogram covers the small places, from the cup to the wax paper around a pastry. Brown and cream carry the system, with powder blue for contrast.

Brand strategy and the visual identity, icons and illustrations. Packaging for coffee and pastry: a sticker label, kraft and printed boxes, a can, a bag, gable pastry boxes, a waxed wrap and a handled bag, with a QR code on the bags and boxes. For the shop: cups, cards, a blade sign, an apron and a cap.

Emilio signed off the final pastry boxes in October 2025: "Everything looks good. Thanks!" By then the client's packaging lead had already chosen the printer he wanted to order from and asked us for the production files.
