## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Mundã is a project of vacation and long-stay homes in La Saladita, on the coast of Guerrero, Mexico. Its full name is Mundã, Aldea del Mar, a village by the sea. The brand had to sell a way of living there, close to the waves and to the neighbors, on posters, on paper and on the wall at the entrance. We made it in 2022 at MK+2, José Pablo's studio before Atla.

The posters carry the line the brand was built on: strengthen your connection with nature, near the waves. Under it sat the values the client chose, connection with nature, fullness, community and a way of life. We kept the wordmark calm, a deep teal serif with a macron over the last A, and let the warmth come from drawings in thin orange line: palms, faces, people holding hands.

The logotype and its variants. Posters with the coastline coordinates, sunset photography and line illustrations. Envelopes, folders and business cards in deep teal, cream and burnt orange. Backlit signage for the entrance, shown in renders on a thatched gate. In 2023, animations of the mark and a looping beach scene.

In 2025 a prospective client wrote to us and named Mundã as the reference for her own brand. What she liked, in her words, was the simplicity of the brand, the people in it and its color palette, which she called interesting.
