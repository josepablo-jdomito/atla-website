## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Reggie makes calming chews, supplements and feeder mats for dogs. The name is short for regimen. New owners from New Zealand bought the company in 2025 and inherited the original brand, a refresh from the year before, and the watered-down version the old team had actually shipped, white jars and all. They wanted to rebuild while flying, moving from direct sales into retail, with a trade show on the calendar.

Simon Doig, co-owner, wrote the problem down: "if we are going to make the change again, we really want to get it right, and produce something special." The refresh looked cleaner and might not stand out on a shelf. The brief ruled out anything overbearing or clinical. The tone had to be the friend who does not judge, and the supplements had to feel like treats.

The idea in our insight work was trust as an emotion: a brand that feels like a relationship, with routine as ritual and science said in a human voice. With the partner studio Vapor and the owners' direction, we brought the logo out stronger, anchored on the brand green, dropped the brown, and gave the mascot fill, shading and bolder lines, so Reggie the dog became an asset again.

Discovery and insight decks comparing the brands in market. A brand identity and its digital expression: wordmark, logo, type, palettes, photography direction and avatar. Website design for the homepage, collection, product, cart and checkout, about, blog, email, pop-ups and a design system. Packaging and labels for the focus products: jars, a pouch, a dropper and the feeder-mat pack.

Ricardo Camargo, of Vapor Studio, when the site went live: "Reggie.com is live and it looks great. Congratulations to the team, great work." The brand was relaunched at SuperZoo in Las Vegas that August.
