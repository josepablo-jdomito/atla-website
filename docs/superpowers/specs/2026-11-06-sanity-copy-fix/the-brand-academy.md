## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
The Branding Academy is José Pablo's weekly newsletter for founders and the people who run brands. It was named at the end of 2025 and runs on Substack. It began as a reply on Instagram: comment academy, and he sends you a branding strategy every week. The identity treats the newsletter as a school: a seal, and a row of bound books in pastel cloth.

The mark is a round seal that stacks the letters A, T and B into one monogram, stamped in gold on cloth book spines of blue, coral, mustard and mint. Line drawings of hands at work carry the illustration. On the phone, the newsletter opens with the idea behind it: branding is a structural decision before it becomes a visual one.

Seal and wordmark, palette, illustration, the newsletter layout, and things to carry it: notebooks, a tote, a cap and cards. Readers sign up through Substack. Sponsors have written asking to advertise in it, and in November 2026 another studio shared it with its own community.
