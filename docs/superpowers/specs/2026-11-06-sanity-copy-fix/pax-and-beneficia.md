## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Pax & Beneficia opened in Las Colinas in 2019 and grew across Dallas-Fort Worth, roasting its own beans. The name means peace and blessings, and the cafés are built on it: a guest is an honored visitor. The company sells roasted coffee and handcrafted syrups online as well as over the counter. A brand existed, and it had fallen behind the mission.

In our reading, specialty coffee is crowded and much of it looks alike. The challenge was an identity bold enough to stand out on a shelf and still true to the café culture that started the company, carried from Turkey and Andalusia to Texas. It also had to work as a system, because new locations kept opening.

The mark is a tree: deep roots, growth outward. Apercu Mono sets the type, in warm earth tones with brighter accents. The company lives by its values, each a pair of P and B words: Prosper & Bond, Persevere & Become, Pause & Breathe, Patience & Belief, Pursue & Beckon. We turned each one into a linocut illustration, so the values travel on the bag instead of staying in a document.

The logo and its animation, an icon, iconography, a pattern and the type system. Packaging: the coffee bag, a box, labels and labels for the syrup bottles. The value illustrations, then applied across cups, totes, cans and shop windows.
