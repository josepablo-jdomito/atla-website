## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Huémac, from Querétaro, set out to be a platform for outdoor experiences: one place to book adventures with the best guides and companies, with safety and trust built in. Its audience ran from the young and adventurous to families and to travellers who wanted the softest version of the outdoors. In 2021 it came to MK+2, José Pablo's studio before Atla, for a strategy and an identity.

The strategy came first. The brand idea was "Con pasión, conexión", with adrenaline, trust and a wide range of activities as its flags. The mission was to make incredible experiences easy through a platform people can rely on. The vision went further: a platform that makes people fall in love with the planet and want to look after it.

The identity is warm and friendly. A yellow-orange serif wordmark sits on forest green, with coral and cream beside it, and a family of smiling mountains and hearts works as mascots on stickers and paper. The tone follows the personality written into the strategy: professional, adventurous, friendly and passionate.

A strategy deck, a moodboard, the graphic master and the identity presentation, then the final brand files at the end of 2021. Letterhead, envelopes, cards, stickers and a T-shirt for the guides, with the brand idea printed on the back.
