## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Sexto Abismo is a gin from San Luis Potosí. The name comes from the Sótano de las Golondrinas in the Huasteca Potosina, one of the deepest abysses on earth. José Pablo started the brand in 2021 with Oscar Gómez and Rodrigo Espinosa, and built its identity at MK+2, his studio before Atla. There was no outside client and no brief, only a bottle to fill and a region to put on the label.

Most gin borrows its look from Britain. Mexico has its own mystique, and Mexican surrealism offered more material than one label could hold. The hard part was choosing. The story on the bottle had to matter as much as the liquid inside it.

The icon is a swallow taking flight from an abyss under an eclipse, a sign of passage from one state to another. The wordmark is a custom organic letterform whose letters change size as they go. The label tells a story: a stranger crosses an abyss, meets La Felina and El Lacayo, drinks something clear and wakes in another world, where the characters watch him.

The name and the story. A brand book, the bottle label and the shipping box. Posters, stickers, postcards, a menu, a billboard, a mural, tent cards for the house cocktails, a trailer and a book for bartenders. In 2025, Atla added a content strategy and a refresh deck for the brand.

The gin went on sale in Mexico in 2022. In 2024 the Beverage Testing Institute gave it a Silver Medal and rated it Highly Recommended.
