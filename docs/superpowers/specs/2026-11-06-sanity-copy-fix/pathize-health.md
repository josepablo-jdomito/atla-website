## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Pathize Health builds an app for people who live with energy-limiting conditions such as Long COVID, ME/CFS and POTS. It reads data from wearables like Apple Health, Fitbit, Garmin and Polar, and helps each person pace the day against a personal limit. In 2023 the team came to MK+2, José Pablo's studio before Atla, on a referral from the founder of Peachy, with a website relaunch close behind.

Alex Bahram wrote the brief. He wanted a mix of serif and sans serif, a strong name and a strong logo that did not clash, colors that felt soft and welcoming without leaning feminine, and illustration alongside lifestyle photography. The team chose the first moodboard and then the first logo, and Lex Oiler wrote back: "We really love the logotype (simple! clean!) and love the heart."

The heart is the icon. It is made of pills that meet, and the break between the lines is what makes it read. Lex noticed it would animate well out of the P of Pathize. Cooper Light sets the titles and Circular the text, on cream, sky blue, mint and mustard. Hand-drawn illustrations and scribbles mark the words that matter, the way a person marks up a document.

Moodboards, the logo, a palette, illustrations and scribbles, a brand book, and applications: business cards, letterhead, the app icon, a pacing screen and pages for the website. The team asked for the raw files and the brand book in a hurry, to finish the relaunch. When it was done, Lex wrote: "We loved working with you guys."
