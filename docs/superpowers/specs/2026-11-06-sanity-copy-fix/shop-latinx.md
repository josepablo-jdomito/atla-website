## description (as it will be stored)

<!-- description:start -->
Shop Latinx is a marketplace for Latino brands in beauty, fashion and lifestyle, founded by Brittany Chavez to give those brands visibility in the North American market. It started as a fashion brand and, as we wrote at the time, it lacked the folklore of the culture it served. In 2022 Brittany brought it to MK+2, José Pablo's studio before Atla, to rebrand it without falling into stereotypes.

The new identity is Y2K: a chrome wordmark on a pink ellipse over a blue and green gradient, airbrushed hibiscus, and an SLX monogram that works as a pill, a necklace and a gold hoop earring. The posters talk to the community directly, with lines like "Seen, supported, and celebrated" and "Guided from above, grounded from below."

María Martínez led the design. The brief, the branding presentation and the reviews ran through July 2022, and then weekly work on social graphics and the Shopify store until the end of the year. The posters describe the marketplace in Brittany's own terms: by us, for us.

That September, Brittany introduced us to Frank Bach, who was "extremely impressed with SLX's new branding", and described MK+2 to contacts at a16z as "the 360 agency that I've been working with."
