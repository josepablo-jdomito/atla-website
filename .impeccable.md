## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are founders, brand leads, and marketing/operations teams evaluating case studies before starting an engagement with Atla. They need to scan quickly, understand quality, and inspect visual execution details without friction.

### Brand Personality
Confident, editorial, precise.
The interface should feel intentional and strategic rather than playful or generic. It should communicate rigor, craft, and clarity.

### Aesthetic Direction
Minimal editorial system with strong typography and image-first storytelling. Neutral surfaces, high contrast, restrained motion, and clear hierarchy. Interactions should feel premium and practical (fast, legible, and keyboard-friendly), not decorative.

### Design Principles
1. Reduce friction before adding flourish: discovery and navigation should always be immediate.
2. Let work lead: photography and project artifacts are the hero; chrome stays quiet.
3. Keep rhythm consistent: spacing, type scale, and interaction density must be systematic.
4. Preserve context across actions: users should never feel disoriented when exploring.
5. Accessibility is baseline: readable contrast, keyboard support, and touch-safe targets.
